Bidadari

HDB's newest flagship estate in District 13, built on the old Bidadari cemetery grounds around Woodleigh and Alkaff, green and planned from scratch, and one of the priciest resale addresses in the HDB world.

What it's like

Brand-new, central and green, Bidadari is where buyers pay million-dollar money for a four-room flat and barely blink.

Bidadari sits in District 13, inside the Toa Payoh planning area, but almost nobody who buys here calls it Toa Payoh. It is HDB's newest flagship estate, launched from 2015 on the grounds of the former Bidadari cemetery and still filling in. HDB sold it as a "community in a garden," and the plan backs that up: Alkaff Lake and Bidadari Park sit at the centre, the flats are laid out in Alkaff-named precincts around them, and an underground bus interchange feeds Woodleigh Village, the estate mall and community club. Everything is new, planned and green in a way the older towns simply are not.

It runs as a mixed estate. The HDB stock is BTO from 2015 onward, and resale has already turned Bidadari into one of the priciest flat addresses on the island. A four-room resale sits around $1.10m, roughly $1,089 psf, and the million-dollar flat is the norm here rather than the headline. Alongside them are new private condos, Park Colonial, The Woodleigh Residences and The Tre Ver among them, with a resale median near $1.53m, about $2,043 psf. A buyer here is really choosing between a premium new flat and a new condo on the same few streets, both bought mainly to live in. Yields are ordinary, near 4.0% on the HDB and 3.0% on a condo.

Getting around is easy. Woodleigh on the North East Line and Bartley on the Circle Line both serve the estate, putting you in town in a short ride, with the Central Expressway and Pan Island Expressway close by for drivers. Potong Pasir is next door, and the schools carry weight: the St Andrew's cluster is on the doorstep, with Cedar Girls and Maris Stella nearby. The honest catch is the price. You pay clearly for new and central here, the estate is still maturing so some amenities are only now arriving, resale flats carry a heavy premium, and from some precincts the MRT is a genuine walk. You buy Bidadari for the newness, the greenery and the address, not for a bargain.

The honest view

What works

  • Brand-new and well-planned, with Alkaff Lake and Bidadari Park at its centre and Woodleigh Village for daily needs.
  • Central and well-connected, with Woodleigh on the North East Line and Bartley on the Circle Line, and a short ride into town.
  • Strong schools close by, including the St Andrew's cluster, Cedar Girls and Maris Stella, right next to Potong Pasir.

Worth weighing

  • Very pricey for HDB, with million-dollar four-room flats the norm and a heavy premium on resale.
  • Still maturing, so some shops, amenities and greenery are only now filling in.
  • From some precincts the MRT is a genuine walk, and yields near 4.0% are ordinary for the money.

这里是什么样

全新、地段居中、绿意怡人，Bidadari 是那种买家花上百万买一间 4 房式组屋也不眨眼的地方。

Bidadari 坐落于 District 13，属 Toa Payoh 规划区，但几乎没有在这里买房的人会说自己住 Toa Payoh。这里是 HDB 最新的旗舰组屋区，从 2015 年开始在前 Bidadari 墓地的地段上开发，目前仍在持续完善中。HDB 将其定位为「花园中的社区」，规划确实名副其实：Alkaff Lake 和 Bidadari Park 居于中央，组屋以 Alkaff 命名的小区环绕四周，地下巴士转换站连通 Woodleigh Village、社区商场和民众联络所。这里的一切都是崭新的、经过规划的、绿意盎然的，是老镇无法比拟的。

整个社区是混合型发展。HDB 组屋是 2015 年起的 BTO，转售市场已经让 Bidadari 成为全岛最贵的组屋地址之一。4 房式转售约 $1.10m，大约 $1,089 psf，百万组屋在这里是常态，不是头条新闻。同时这里也有新私宅，包括 Park Colonial、The Woodleigh Residences 和 The Tre Ver 等，转售中位价约 $1.53m，大约 $2,043 psf。在这里买房，其实是在同几条街上做选择：买顶价新组屋还是买新 condo，两者都以自住为主。租金回报率普通，HDB 约 4.0%，condo 约 3.0%。

出行相当便利。Woodleigh 站（东北线）和 Bartley 站（环线）都服务这个区，几站就能进城，开车走 Central Expressway 和 Pan Island Expressway 也很顺畅。旁边就是 Potong Pasir，学校阵容也很亮眼：St Andrew's 群校近在咫尺，Cedar Girls 和 Maris Stella 也在附近。说实话，价格是最大的门槛。在这里买房，明显要为「新」和「中心地段」付出溢价，社区仍在成熟中，部分配套才刚落地，转售组屋溢价不低，从某些小区走去 MRT 也要一段路。买 Bidadari，买的是新、绿和地址，不是捡便宜。

适合谁想要全新、规划有序、地段居中又绿意充足的住宅区，同时能够接受百万组屋或新 condo 价位的家庭。

老实说

好在哪里

  • 全新规划，配套齐全，Alkaff Lake 和 Bidadari Park 坐镇中央，Woodleigh Village 满足日常所需。
  • 地点居中，交通四通八达，Woodleigh 在东北线，Bartley 在环线，进城只需一小段路程。
  • 名校近在咫尺，包括 St Andrew's 系列学校、Cedar Girls 和 Maris Stella，紧邻 Potong Pasir。

需要权衡

  • HDB 价格偏贵，四房式百万组屋已是常态，转售溢价相当重。
  • 市镇仍在成长中，部分商店、设施和绿化目前才陆续到位。
  • 有些地段走到 MRT 确实有段路，加上价格不低，约 4.0% 的租金回报只能算是一般水平。
