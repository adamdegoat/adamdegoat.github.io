What it's like

Dawson is where Singapore rebuilt public housing as architecture, and where a well-designed HDB flat now trades past a million dollars a short walk from the train.

Dawson is the poster child for what the government can do when it rebuilds a public housing estate from scratch. The striking blocks here, SkyVille @ Dawson and SkyTerrace @ Dawson, are design-award HDB with sky gardens, communal terraces and forms you will not see in an ordinary town, and they replaced the old Tanglin Halt flats, the low-rise Chap Lau Chu blocks that were among the first HDB ever built. Some of those older Tanglin Halt blocks are still being cleared and redeveloped under the SERS renewal scheme, so parts of the estate are new, parts are a worksite, and the whole place carries the sense of a town being remade in real time.

The housing is not one thing, which is what makes Dawson interesting to price. The flagship HDB flats are genuinely sought after, and million-dollar resale is normal here rather than a headline, with four-room flats around $1.02m, close to $1,084 per square foot. On top of Queenstown MRT sits a cluster of new private condos, Stirling Residences, Commonwealth Towers, Queens Peak and Margaret Ville among them, where a resale unit runs about $1.59m at roughly $2,289 per square foot. So a design-award flat and a brand-new condo can sit within sight of each other, priced a long way apart, and both command a premium for the address.

Location is the quiet engine under all of it. Queenstown on the East-West Line puts you a few stops from the CBD, and one-north, the science and media district, is close enough to walk or take one stop. Day to day, Queensway Shopping Centre, Anchorpoint and IKEA Alexandra are nearby, and the Queenstown and Margaret Drive amenities cover the basics. The honest catch is the price and the timing. Million-dollar HDB is a real stretch for most buyers, some of the older Tanglin Halt flats carry shorter remaining leases, redevelopment and construction are still going on around the estate, and the new condos are compact and pricey per square foot. You buy in Dawson for a central, renewed address, not for space or a bargain.

Who it suitsBuyers who want a central, well-designed HDB close to town and can stretch to a million-dollar flat, or a new condo sitting right on the MRT.

The honest view

What works

  • Design-award public housing, SkyVille and SkyTerrace @ Dawson, with sky gardens and communal terraces you will not find in an ordinary HDB town.
  • Central and well-connected, with Queenstown MRT on the East-West Line a few stops from the CBD and one-north close by, plus Queensway, Anchorpoint and IKEA Alexandra nearby.
  • A renewed estate with strong, steady demand, so both the flagship flats and the new MRT condos hold their prices firmly.

Worth weighing

  • Million-dollar HDB is normal here, which is a real stretch for most buyers.
  • Some of the older Tanglin Halt flats carry shorter remaining leases.
  • Redevelopment and construction are still going on around the estate, and the new condos are compact and pricey per square foot.

这里是什么样

Dawson 是新加坡把公共组屋做成建筑艺术的地方，设计精良的 HDB 单位如今转售价格已突破百万元，步行几分钟就到地铁站。

Dawson 完美展示了政府从头重建一个组屋区能做出什么成绩。这里两栋造型抢眼的楼，SkyVille @ Dawson 和 SkyTerrace @ Dawson，都是得过设计奖的 HDB，配有空中花园、公共露台，外形在普通市镇根本看不到。这两个项目取代了旧日 Tanglin Halt 的组屋，也就是那批俗称 Chap Lau Chu 的低层楼宇，它们是新加坡最早期建成的 HDB 之一。部分旧 Tanglin Halt 楼宇目前仍在 SERS 计划下陆续清拆重建，所以这一带有的地方崭新，有的还是工地，整个区都透着一股市镇正在实时蜕变的感觉。

这里的住宅类型不只一种，这正是 Dawson 在定价上耐人寻味的地方。旗舰 HDB 单位真的非常抢手，百万元转售在这里是常态，不是新闻，四房式单位约 $1.02m，接近每平方尺 $1,084。Queenstown MRT 站上方一带聚集了数个新私人公寓，包括 Stirling Residences、Commonwealth Towers、Queens Peak 与 Margaret Ville，转售单位约 $1.59m，折合约每平方尺 $2,289。所以，一个获奖组屋单位与一个全新私人公寓可以近在咫尺，价格却相差甚远，两者都因地段而享有溢价。

地段才是背后真正的驱动力。East-West Line 上的 Queenstown 站距离 CBD 只需几站，one-north 科学与媒体区近到可以步行或搭一站到达。日常生活方面，Queensway Shopping Centre、Anchorpoint 和 IKEA Alexandra 都在附近，Queenstown 和 Margaret Drive 的配套也涵盖了基本所需。说实话，最大的顾虑在于价格和时机。百万元 HDB 对大多数买家来说真的很吃力，部分较旧的 Tanglin Halt 组屋剩余租约较短，屋苑周边的重建和施工仍在进行，而新私人公寓则单位细、呎价高。在 Dawson 买房，买的是中心地段的焕新地址，不是空间，也不是实惠。

适合谁适合想要靠近市区、设计精良的 HDB 且预算能伸展到百万元组屋的买家，或者考虑紧邻 MRT 新私人公寓的买家。

老实说

好在哪里

  • 获设计大奖的 HDB 楼盘，SkyVille 和 SkyTerrace @ Dawson，有空中花园和公共露台，这些在普通 HDB 市镇里根本找不到。
  • 地段中心，交通方便，Queenstown 东西线 MRT 离市区几站路，one-north 就在旁边，Queensway、Anchorpoint 和 IKEA Alexandra 也近在咫尺。
  • 更新后的市镇需求稳健持续，旗舰组屋和 MRT 上盖新 condo 的价格都很稳。

需要权衡

  • 这里百万组屋已是常态，对大多数买家来说确实很吃力。
  • 部分较旧的 Tanglin Halt 组屋剩余租约年限偏短。
  • 市镇周边重建施工仍在继续，新 condo 面积不大，每平方英尺的价格也不便宜。
