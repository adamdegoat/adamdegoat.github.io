Clementi

A mature west town built around schools and jobs, with NUS and NUS High next door, one-north and Science Park a short hop away, and Clementi Mall sitting right over the MRT.

What it's like

Clementi trades on two things buyers pay up for: schools and proximity to work.

Clementi is one of the older west towns, settled since the 1970s and long fully built out. Its pull has always been academic. NUS and NUS High School sit right next door, and the town itself holds Nan Hua High, Nan Hua Primary and Pei Tong, names that shape where young families want to register. one-north and Science Park are a short hop away, so a lot of residents work within a few train stops of home. Clementi Mall sits directly over the MRT station, 321 Clementi anchors a second retail cluster, and there are wet markets and coffee shops threaded through the older estates. For a west town it is unusually complete.

The housing is a mix that has drifted upward. Resale HDB dominates, and it is on the pricier side for the region: a four-room now runs a median around $840k, with a run of million-dollar flats in the newer blocks near the MRT. Buyers are largely families chasing the school queue and staff who want a short commute to NUS, one-north or the Science Park, and that steady demand is why prices firm rather than drift. Private buyers get a smaller pool, mostly newer launches like Clavon and Parc Clematis, where the condo median sits near $1.69m, about $1,674 psf. Both flats and condos change hands on the same story: schools plus jobs.

Transport is a real strength. Clementi sits on the East-West Line, so the city fringe and one-north are quick, and the AYE and Clementi Road put drivers on the western expressways fast. The honest catch is price and age. Clementi is not cheap for the west, so you pay a premium the moment you cross into the district, and a good share of the flats are genuinely old, which means shorter leases to weigh against financing and a long hold. You are buying the address and the schools, and you pay for both.

The honest view

What works

  • One of the strongest school and jobs draws in the west: NUS, NUS High and Nan Hua next door, one-north and Science Park close.
  • Genuinely convenient, with Clementi Mall built over the MRT and the East-West Line putting the city fringe within reach.
  • A firm HDB yield near 5.6% on a four-room, high for a mature, well-located town.

Worth weighing

  • Not cheap for the west: you pay a clear premium the moment you buy into the district.
  • Many flats are genuinely old, so shorter leases weigh on financing and a long hold.
  • Condos sit near $1.69m for modest yields around 3.2%, a big step up from the flats.

这里是什么样

Clementi 最让买家愿意多付钱的，就是两样东西：学校和工作地点近。

Clementi 是西部较老的市镇之一，七十年代就已发展，早已全面建成。这里一直以学术著称。NUS 和 NUS High School 就在旁边，市镇本身也有 Nan Hua High、Nan Hua Primary 和 Pei Tong，这些名字影响着不少年轻家庭的选区决定。one-north 和 Science Park 就在几站之遥，不少居民上班地点离家很近。Clementi Mall 直接建在 MRT 站上方，321 Clementi 是另一个主要商场聚点，旧组屋区里还有湿巴刹和咖啡店穿插其中。以西部市镇来说，Clementi 的配套算是相当齐全的。

这里的住宅结构偏高端化。转售 HDB 是主力，在西部算是较贵的：四房式中位价现在约 $840k，MRT 附近的新组屋里有不少百万元楼盘。买家以追学校名额的家庭，以及在 NUS、one-north 或 Science Park 上班、想短途通勤的人为主，这种稳定的需求撑住了价格。私宅选择较少，主要是 Clavon 和 Parc Clematis 等较新项目，condo 中位价约 $1.69m，约 $1,674 psf。HDB 和 condo 的买家，看的都是同一个故事：学校加工作。

交通是 Clementi 真正的强项。这里在东西线上，到市中心边缘和 one-north 都很方便；AYE 和 Clementi Road 也让驾车人士能快速上西部高速公路。说实话，代价在于价格和楼龄。Clementi 在西部不便宜，一进这个区就有溢价，而且不少组屋楼龄已高，剩余租约短，买的时候要考虑融资和长期持有的问题。买这里，买的是地段和学区，两样都要付出代价。

适合谁想要争学校名额的家庭，以及在 NUS、one-north 或 Science Park 上班、希望短途通勤、不介意支付西部溢价的人士。

老实说

好在哪里

  • 西部地区学校和就业吸引力数一数二：NUS、NUS High 和南华就在隔壁，one-north 和 Science Park 也近在咫尺。
  • 生活真的很方便，Clementi Mall 直接建在MRT上方，东西线让市区边缘触手可及。
  • HDB 四房租金回报率接近 5.6%，对一个地段成熟、位置好的市镇来说，这个数字相当不错。

需要权衡

  • 在西部来说也绝对不便宜：买进这个区的那一刻，就得明显付出溢价。
  • 很多组屋楼龄确实不小，剩余租约较短，对申请贷款和长期持有都会有压力。
  • 私人 condo 价格接近 $1.69m，租金回报率约 3.2%，比组屋贵了一大截。
