Punggol

Singapore's youngest waterfront town, built around the Punggol Waterway and Waterway Point, with the newest flats and long leases, and a digital district still taking shape.

What it's like

Punggol is the country's newest town, and it feels it: young flats, water on the doorstep, and a family-heavy crowd.

Punggol was farmland and fishing kampongs until the 2000s, so almost everything you see went up in the last twenty years. The town is built around the Punggol Waterway, a man-made canal ringed by parkland, cycling paths and rows of newer HDB blocks. Waterway Point, the main mall, sits directly above the MRT and LRT interchange, so the shops, the food court and the train are all under one roof. Punggol Point and Coney Island sit at the northern edge for weekends by the sea. It is a genuinely new-town feel, planned and green rather than lived-in and worn.

The housing skews young and family-heavy. This is one of the biggest BTO catchments in Singapore, so most flats are well under twenty years old with leases in the 90s, and the resale market is dominated by four and five-room flats bought by first-time couples. Private stock is mostly executive condos and mass-market condos like Watertown above the station, A Treasure Trove, Waterbay and Twin Waterfalls, priced in the $1m to $1.7m band rather than anything luxury. The result is a town full of young kids, prams and primary schools, without the older resale flats you find in the mature estates.

The trade-off is location. Punggol sits right in the far north-east corner, so the ride into Raffles Place runs the better part of an hour, and you lean on the North-East Line plus the Punggol LRT loops, which have had their share of reliability grumbles over the years. That is changing: the North-East Line now runs one stop further to Punggol Coast, the terminus that serves the Punggol Digital District and the SIT campus, and the Cross Island Line is coming too, which should bring jobs and a second rail link closer to home. For now you buy Punggol for the new flats, the water and the long lease, and you accept a longer commute as the price of it.

Who it suitsYoung families who want a brand-new flat with a long lease and water on the doorstep, and are willing to trade a longer commute for it.

The honest view

What works

  • The newest flats in Singapore, most with leases in the 90s, which matters for financing and a long hold.
  • Water on the doorstep: the Punggol Waterway, Punggol Point and Coney Island are all within the town.
  • A healthy HDB rental yield near 5.6% on a 4-room, and entry prices at about $679 psf that sit below the national average.

Worth weighing

  • A real commute into the central districts, this is the far north-east corner.
  • You lean heavily on the North-East Line and the Punggol LRT loops, which have had reliability grumbles.
  • Some of the promised amenities, the digital district and the new rail links, are still being built out.

Punggol

新加坡最年轻的滨水市镇，以 Punggol Waterway 和 Waterway Point 为核心，拥有全岛最新的组屋和最长的地契，数字商业区也还在持续成形当中。

这里是什么样

Punggol是新加坡最新的市镇，感觉也的确如此：组屋新、家门口有水景，住的大多是年轻家庭。

Punggol在2000年代之前还是农地和渔村甘榜，所以现在看到的几乎所有东西，都是过去二十年内建起来的。整个市镇围绕Punggol Waterway而建，这条人工运河四周是公园绿地、脚踏车道，以及一排排较新的HDB组屋。主要商场Waterway Point就在MRT和LRT交汇站的正上方，购物、美食广场和地铁全在同一屋檐下。Punggol Point和Coney Island在市镇北端，是周末到海边散心的好去处。这里有种真正的新市镇感，规划有序、绿意盎然，没有那种历经岁月留下的烟火气。

这里的住宅偏年轻、家庭人口多。Punggol是新加坡最大的BTO申请区之一，大多数组屋楼龄不到二十年，剩余租约还有90多年，转售市场以四房和五房为主，买家多是首次置业的年轻夫妇。私宅方面主要是executive condo和大众化condo，例如站上方的Watertown、A Treasure Trove、Waterbay和Twin Waterfalls，价位在$1m至$1.7m之间，没有什么豪华项目。整个市镇到处是小孩、婴儿车和小学，跟成熟市镇里那些楼龄较高的转售组屋很不一样。

要取舍的是地理位置。Punggol位于最东北角，搭地铁到Raffles Place要将近一个小时，主要靠North-East Line加上Punggol LRT两条环线，而LRT这些年来的可靠性也让不少人有怨言。不过情况正在改变：North-East Line现已延伸多一站到Punggol Coast，这个终点站服务Punggol Digital District和SIT校园；Cross Island Line也即将到来，预计会带来更多工作机会，同时让家门口多一条铁路选择。现阶段买Punggol，是冲着新组屋、水景和长租约，而较长的通勤时间，则是得接受的代价。

适合谁想要全新组屋、租约长、家门口有水景，同时愿意接受较长通勤时间的年轻家庭。

老实说

好在哪里

  • 新加坡最新的组屋，大部分剩余租约都有九十多年，对贷款申请和长期持有都很有利。
  • 水景就在家门口：Punggol Waterway、Punggol Point 和 Coney Island 都在镇内。
  • HDB 四房式的租金回报率约5.6%，表现不错，入手价格约$679 psf，低于全国平均水平。

需要权衡

  • 这里位于最东北角，要进中部地区确实得花不少时间通勤。
  • 这里主要靠东北线和 Punggol LRT 环线出行，这两条线都曾有过可靠性方面的投诉。
  • 部分承诺中的设施，包括数码园区和新的地铁线，目前仍在建设当中。
