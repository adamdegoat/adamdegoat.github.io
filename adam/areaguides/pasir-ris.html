Pasir Ris

A relaxed coastal town at the far eastern end, built around Pasir Ris Park and the beach, with White Sands and a new integrated town centre at its core.

What it's like

Pasir Ris trades the commute for the coast, and plenty of families take that deal.

Pasir Ris sits at the far eastern end of Singapore, and its character is coastal and unhurried in a way most of the island isn't. Pasir Ris Park runs along the northern edge for kilometres, with a real beach, a mangrove boardwalk, cycling paths and barbecue pits that fill up every weekend. The name means white sand, and the town has always leaned into that: this is where east-side families come to slow down, and where Downtown East, E!Hub and the Wild Wild Wet water park draw crowds from across the region.

The housing here is mostly HDB, with a settled, family-first feel rather than a young-professional edge. Four-room resale flats change hands around $638k, and the town has been drawing buyers who want space, greenery and the beach close by, and are willing to sit further out for it. The private stock is a coastal band along the waterfront, developments like NV Residences, Ripple Bay, Coco Palms and Livia, most of them 2010s launches priced around the $1.3m to $1.8m mark. The new Pasir Ris Central redevelopment, an integrated bus interchange, mall and homes rising next to White Sands, is reshaping the town centre and adding fresh private supply on top of the existing mall.

Getting around is straightforward within the east and slower into town. Pasir Ris is the eastern terminus of the East-West Line, and the Cross Island Line is adding an interchange here, which will open up more of the island without a change at the city end. Changi Airport is only minutes away, and the TPE gives drivers a clean run north and west. The honest catch is the one the map makes obvious: you are at the very edge, so the ride into the central business district is a long one, around forty minutes on the train. The coast and the greenery are the trade you make for that distance.

The honest view

What works

  • The coastal-but-affordable mix rents well, a four-room grosses roughly 6.2%.
  • The coast on your doorstep: Pasir Ris Park, a real beach, the mangrove boardwalk and Downtown East.
  • Changi Airport is minutes away, and the Cross Island Line will widen where the town connects to.

Worth weighing

  • A long commute into the central districts, around 40 minutes by train from the far east.
  • Mostly older HDB stock, so lease decay matters for financing and a long hold.
  • Condos are a big step up in price for modest yields near 3.4%.

Pasir Ris

东端最远处轻松惬意的海滨市镇，围绕Pasir Ris Park和海滩而建，White Sands和全新综合市镇中心就在核心位置。

这里是什么样

Pasir Ris 用通勤时间换来海岸线，很多家庭都觉得这笔账划算。

Pasir Ris 坐落在新加坡最东端，那种悠闲的海滨气质是岛上大多数地方都没有的。Pasir Ris Park 沿北边延伸好几公里，有真正的沙滩、红树林木栈道、脚踏车道和每逢周末都坐满人的烧烤区。地名本身就是白沙的意思，这个市镇一直都是这个调子：东边的家庭来这里放慢脚步，Downtown East、E!Hub 和 Wild Wild Wet 水上乐园也吸引了整个区域的人潮。

这里的住宅以 HDB 为主，整体氛围成熟稳重，以家庭为重心，不是那种年轻专业人士聚集的地方。四房式转售组屋成交价约在 $638k 左右，吸引的是那些看重空间、绿化和海滩、愿意住远一点的买家。私宅集中在沿海一带，包括 NV Residences、Ripple Bay、Coco Palms 和 Livia 等项目，大多是 2010 年代推出的，价格在 $1.3m 到 $1.8m 之间。Pasir Ris Central 综合重建项目，涵盖巴士转换站、商场和住宅，正在 White Sands 旁边拔地而起，重新塑造市镇中心，也在原有商场基础上新增了私宅供应。

在东部一带出行很方便，进城则要花多点时间。Pasir Ris 是 East-West Line 的东端终点站，Cross Island Line 也将在这里设转换站，届时不用在市区换车就能到达岛上更多地方。Changi Airport 就在几分钟车程内，TPE 也让开车往北或往西都畅通无阻。说实在的，地图已经说明了一切：这里在最边缘，坐地铁进中央商业区大概要四十分钟。海岸线和绿化，就是你为这段距离换来的代价。

适合谁适合看重海滩、公园和空间、不介意通勤时间长的家庭，住在最东边也无所谓的买家。

老实说

好在哪里

  • 滨海兼实惠的组合，租市表现不俗，四房式单位毛回报率约 6.2%。
  • 海岸就在门口：Pasir Ris Park、真正的沙滩、红树林木栈道，还有 Downtown East。
  • Changi Airport 几分钟即达，Cross Island Line 通车后将大幅扩展这一市镇的连通网络。

需要权衡

  • 通勤到中部地区路途较长，从东端乘地铁约需 40 分钟。
  • 大多数是较旧的 HDB 单位，长期持有时地契剩余年限对贷款融资影响不可忽视。
  • condo 价格贵出一大截，但租金回报率仅约 3.4%，相对有限。
