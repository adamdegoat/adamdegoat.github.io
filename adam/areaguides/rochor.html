Rochor

The eclectic city fringe just north of the CBD, where Bugis shopping, Kampong Glam, Little India and the Bras Basah arts belt sit almost on top of each other in District 7.

What it's like

Central, characterful and unapologetically urban, Rochor is where new mixed-use towers share the street with old shophouses and some of the most connected ground in Singapore.

Rochor sits just north of the central business district, covering the run from Bugis and Rochor across to the edge of Little India and the Kampong Glam quarter around Sultan Mosque and Haji Lane. The housing is mostly private, and much of it is recent: mixed-use launches like Duo Residences, Midtown Modern and The M dropped tall glass towers straight into a low-rise district of shophouses and older walk-ups. There are small, older HDB pockets around Rochor and Selegie, but they are the exception rather than the rule, so this reads as a private, city-fringe market with pricing to match.

What people buy into is the location and the street life. Within a short walk you have Bugis Street and Bugis Junction for shopping, Kampong Glam and Haji Lane for cafes, bars and independent shops, Little India and Mustafa Centre a few minutes north, and the Bras Basah arts and museum belt to the south. It is loud, dense and full of character, packed with food from a dozen cuisines, and it pulls in singles, couples and expats who want to be in the middle of the action and can do without a big flat or a quiet street. This is a lifestyle and connectivity address, not a family-suburb one.

Connectivity is the strongest card here. Bugis is an interchange on the East-West and Downtown Lines, with Rochor on the Downtown Line, Jalan Besar close by and Bencoolen on the same line to the south, so several lines sit within a short walk and the CBD is minutes away. The honest catch is the setting. Greenery is limited, this is genuinely city-fringe ground with few real parks, and you live with heavy tourist and nightlife foot traffic. The stock is a real mix of shiny new launches and tired older buildings, and units in the newer towers tend to be compact. You buy here for the central position, the transport and the street life, not for space or quiet.

Who it suitsSingles, couples and expats who want a central, hyper-connected address with real street life and character, and don't need a big flat, greenery or a quiet neighbourhood.

The honest view

What works

  • Some of the most connected ground in Singapore, with Bugis interchange on two lines and Rochor, Jalan Besar and Bencoolen all close by.
  • Street life and food on the doorstep, from Kampong Glam and Haji Lane to Little India, Mustafa and the Bras Basah arts belt.
  • A genuinely central, walkable position, with the CBD, Marina and the museum district all a few minutes away.

Worth weighing

  • Dense and urban with little greenery, so few real parks and not much quiet.
  • Heavy tourist and nightlife foot traffic, which means noise and crowds at the busier edges.
  • A real mix of shiny new launches and tired older stock, and units in the newer towers tend to be compact.

这里是什么样

Rochor 地段正中心，个性十足，都市感一点都不遮掩，新建的综合用途高楼和老店屋并肩而立，是全新加坡交通连接最密集的地方之一。

Rochor 紧挨着 CBD 北边，从 Bugis、Rochor 一路延伸到 Little India 边缘，以及 Sultan Mosque 和 Haji Lane 一带的 Kampong Glam 区。这里的住宅以私宅为主，而且有不少是新项目：Duo Residences、Midtown Modern 和 The M 这些综合用途楼盘，把一栋栋高耸的玻璃大楼直接嵌进了老店屋和旧楼林立的低层街区。Rochor 和 Selegie 一带还有少量较旧的 HDB，但只是少数。整体来说，这里是个以私宅为主的城市边缘市场，价格也相应水涨船高。

买这里，买的是地段和街头生活。步行就能到 Bugis Street 和 Bugis Junction 购物，Kampong Glam 和 Haji Lane 有咖啡馆、酒吧和特色小店，往北几分钟是 Little India 和 Mustafa Centre，往南则是 Bras Basah 的艺术与博物馆地带。这里热闹、密集，充满个性，各种美食应有尽有，吸引的是那些想融入城市节奏、不需要大单位、也不介意街道喧嚣的单身、情侣和外籍人士。这是一个靠生活方式和交通便利取胜的地址，不是那种适合家庭定居的郊区选择。

这里最大的王牌就是交通。Bugis 是 East-West Line 和 Downtown Line 的转换站，Downtown Line 上还有 Rochor 站，附近有 Jalan Besar，南边是 Bencoolen，好几条线路步行都能到，去 CBD 只需几分钟。说实话，缺点也很现实。绿化不多，这是真正的城市边缘地带，几乎没有像样的公园，观光客和夜生活带来的人流也是日常的一部分。楼盘新旧混杂，新项目光鲜亮眼，旧楼则已显老态，新大楼的单位也普遍偏小。买这里，靠的是地段、交通和街头氛围，不是空间，也不是宁静。

适合谁适合想要地段中心、交通超便利、又有真实街头生活和个性的单身、情侣和外籍人士，前提是不需要大单位、绿化或安静社区。

老实说

好在哪里

  • 这里的交通在全新加坡数一数二，Bugis转乘站贯通两条地铁线，Rochor、Jalan Besar和Bencoolen也都在咫尺之间。
  • 出门就是街边热闹和美食，从Kampong Glam、Haji Lane，到小印度、Mustafa，再到Bras Basah文艺地带，应有尽有。
  • 地段真的很中心，步行就方便，CBD、Marina湾和博物馆区几分钟就到了。

需要权衡

  • 密集都市，绿化少，真正的公园不多，也没什么清静的地方。
  • 游客和夜生活人流都旺，较热闹的路段难免噪音大、人也挤。
  • 新旧楼盘混杂，新推出的项目光鲜亮丽，老楼有些显旧，新高楼里的单位普遍偏小。
