Area guide · North-East

Hougang

One of the north-east's biggest and most established heartland towns, with a strong local identity, its own mall and food, and the North-East Line running through it, with a Cross Island Line interchange on the way.

3 NEL stationsHDB from $622kCondo median $1.29mYield 6.2%
What it's like

Hougang is old-school heartland north-east, settled, food-mad and cheaper than the newer estates around it.

Hougang is one of the larger and older HDB towns in the north-east, built up from the 1980s and now fully mature. It has kept a distinct local identity that a lot of the newer estates around it never developed. Hougang Mall anchors the town centre next to the bus interchange, with wet markets, coffee shops and the Kovan Hougang food centre filling in the gaps. The town is known first and foremost for its food, and it has the settled, unhurried feel of an estate where families have stayed put for decades rather than a place people are just passing through.

The housing here is mostly HDB, and a good chunk of it is older stock with leases that have been running a while, which is exactly why the prices sit below the shinier towns nearby. A four-room resale runs around $622k, well under what you would pay in the newer parts of Sengkang or Punggol. There is private property too, clustered around Kovan and along the Serangoon river, projects like The Florence Residences, Riverfront Residences, Rio Vista and the older Kovan Melody, but the town reads as a working heartland estate rather than a condo belt. It draws families who want space and a real neighbourhood at a price that still makes sense.

Transport is decent for the north-east. The North-East Line stops at Hougang, Kovan and Buangkok, and the Cross Island Line will add an interchange at Hougang that will make the town a lot better connected across the island. The honest catch is the one the north-east has always carried: you sit a fair way out, so the ride into the city on the NEL runs a good half hour, and a lot of the flat stock is on the older side, which matters for financing and for anyone planning a long hold.

Who it suitsFamilies who want a settled heartland town and strong food at a lower price than the newer north-east estates, and can live with a longer commute and older flats.

The honest view

What works

  • Rents hold up well against the modest entry price, a four-room grosses about 6.2%.
  • Real value for the north-east: a four-room here runs well below the newer estates nearby.
  • Good local life, with Hougang Mall, wet markets and some of the better heartland food in the north-east.

Worth weighing

  • A long commute into the central districts, the NEL ride into town runs about half an hour.
  • Much of the flat stock is older, with shorter leases that matter for financing and a long hold.
  • Fewer newer private launches than Sengkang or Punggol if you want a modern condo.

小区指南 · 东北部

Hougang

东北部最大、最成熟的组屋市镇之一，地方认同感强，有自己的商场和美食，东北线贯穿其中，跨岛线转换站也在路上了。

这里是什么样

Hougang 是东北部那种老派组屋区，生活踏实，出了名的爱吃，价格也比周围那些新市镇便宜。

Hougang 是东北部规模较大、历史较长的 HDB 市镇之一，从 1980 年代开始发展，如今已完全成熟。这里保留了鲜明的地方特色，是周围很多新市镇都没有的。Hougang Mall 就在巴士转换站旁边，是市镇中心的核心地标，湿巴刹、咖啡店和 Kovan Hougang 熟食中心把生活配套填得满满的。这个市镇最出名的就是吃，整体氛围沉稳踏实、不慌不忙，是那种家庭扎根了几十年的地方，不是人们路过的中转站。

这里的住宅以 HDB 为主，有相当一部分是楼龄较高的旧组屋，剩余地契也已经跑了一段时间了，这正是为什么价格比周围那些光鲜的市镇低。四房式转售组屋约 $622k，比 Sengkang 或 Punggol 的新区便宜不少。私宅也有，主要集中在 Kovan 一带和 Serangoon 河沿岸，像 The Florence Residences、Riverfront Residences、Rio Vista，还有楼龄较高的 Kovan Melody，不过这个市镇整体上给人的感觉还是一个接地气的组屋区，不是那种 condo 扎堆的地段。吸引的是那些想要空间感和真实邻里氛围、价格又还在合理范围内的家庭。

交通对东北部来说算不错了。North-East Line 在 Hougang、Kovan 和 Buangkok 都有站，Cross Island Line 还会在 Hougang 设立转换站，到时候整个市镇的横向连接会大幅提升。说实话，东北部一直都有这个问题：位置偏，乘 NEL 进城大概要半小时，而且很多组屋楼龄都不小了，这对贷款融资和打算长期持有的买家都是要考虑的事情。

适合谁适合想要成熟组屋区、吃的选择丰富、价格比东北新市镇低，又能接受通勤时间较长、组屋楼龄偏高的家庭。

老实说

好在哪里

  • 相对于不算高的入场价，租金表现很不错，四房式毛租金回报约 6.2%。
  • 东北部真正的性价比所在：这里的四房式价格远低于附近的新市镇。
  • 本地生活配套齐全，有 Hougang Mall、湿巴刹，还有东北部数一数二的组屋区美食。

需要权衡

  • 进中央区通勤距离较长，乘 NEL 进城约半小时。
  • 这里大多数组屋都比较旧，剩余租约较短，申请贷款和长期持有都要留意。
  • 跟Sengkang或Punggol比，新私宅项目少一些，想买新condo选择没那么多。
