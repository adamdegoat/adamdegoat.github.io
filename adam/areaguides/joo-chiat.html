Neighbourhood in Marine Parade

Joo Chiat

Singapore's first Heritage Town, a long spine of conserved Peranakan shophouses off Katong in District 15, almost entirely private, with no HDB and a food strip that runs late.

What it's like

The country's first conservation area, bought for its shophouses, its food and its address rather than for facilities or a family flat.

Joo Chiat was named Singapore's first Heritage Town in 2011, and its Peranakan shophouses have been a gazetted conservation area since 1993. The whole place is built around that heritage. The long spine of Joo Chiat Road is lined with ornate Peranakan shophouses, and the side streets hold more of the same, with the row of pastel terraces on Koon Seng Road doing the postcard duty. It sits inside the Marine Parade planning area, next to Katong and a short way from East Coast, and it carries a District 15 postcode. The heritage here is Peranakan and Eurasian, and you can still read it in the temples, the churches and the food.

The housing is private from top to bottom. There is essentially no HDB in Joo Chiat, so buyers are choosing between conserved shophouses, older walk-up apartments, a handful of small freehold condominiums and pockets of landed. The shophouses are the trophy stock, many of them now running as cafes, restaurants, bars or boutique stays rather than homes, and they trade at heritage prices with restoration rules attached. The condos are smaller and older on average, so family-sized modern stock is thin. A resale condo here sits around a $1.77m median, near $1,707 per square foot, with gross yields around 3.3%, healthier than the prime districts because the entry price is lower and rental demand is steady.

What pulls people in is character and food more than facilities. Joo Chiat Road is a dense run of Eurasian and Peranakan kitchens, Vietnamese eateries, old kopitiams and a newer layer of bars, and it draws a younger crowd, creatives and expats who would rather live over a good coffee than next to a mall. The Thomson-East Coast Line now serves the area at Marine Parade and Marine Terrace, which finally put it on the rail map. Be clear-eyed on the trade-offs. The best shophouses are expensive and tied up in conservation rules, most units are small or older, the weekend and late-night crowds are real, and there is no mall of its own, so you lean on Katong and i12 next door. Parts of the Joo Chiat and Geylang fringe once carried a nightlife and red-light reputation; that has largely given way to gentrification, but it is worth knowing the history.

The honest view

What works

  • Real character and history, a whole neighbourhood of conserved Peranakan shophouses and Singapore's first designated Heritage Town.
  • One of the best food streets in Singapore on the doorstep, from Eurasian and Peranakan kitchens to Vietnamese eateries, old kopitiams and late bars.
  • Now on the rail map, with the Thomson-East Coast Line at Marine Parade and Marine Terrace, and Katong, i12 and East Coast Park all close by.
  • Yields around 3.3% gross are healthier than the prime central districts, on steady rental demand from a younger, expat crowd.

Worth weighing

  • Conserved shophouses are expensive and come with restoration rules, so the trophy stock is not a casual buy.
  • Most units are small or older, and family-sized modern stock is limited, so it suits singles, couples and investors more than large families.
  • Weekend crowds and some late-night noise come with the food and bar scene, and parts of the fringe carry an older nightlife reputation.
  • No mall of its own, so everyday shopping leans on Katong and i12 next door, and there is no HDB option to fall back on.

这里是什么样

全国首个保护区，买家看中的是 shophouses、美食和这个地址，而不是为了配套设施或家庭式单位。

Joo Chiat 在 2011 年被列为新加坡首个 Heritage Town，区内的 Peranakan shophouses 自 1993 年起便已划为法定保护区。整个社区都是围绕这份传承而生的。Joo Chiat Road 这条主干道两旁排列着精雕细琢的 Peranakan shophouses，周边小巷也是同样的风景，Koon Seng Road 那排粉彩色排屋更是明信片级别的经典一景。这里属于 Marine Parade 规划区，紧邻 Katong，离 East Coast 也不远，邮区是 District 15。这一带的文化遗产以 Peranakan 和 Eurasian 为主，从庙宇、教堂到街边美食，处处都留有印记。

这里的住宅全属私人产业，基本上没有 HDB。买家要选的是保护区 shophouses、旧式步梯公寓、小量小型永久地契 condo，或零星的有地住宅。Shophouses 是最抢手的资产，很多已改作咖啡馆、餐厅、酒吧或精品住宿，而非自住用途，以遗产价位成交，并附带修缮规定。整体来看，condo 普遍偏小偏旧，适合家庭的现代大单位不多。这里的二手 condo 中位价约 $1.77m，约 $1,707 psf，毛租金回报率约 3.3%，比核心地段更健康，因为入场价较低，租赁需求也稳定。

吸引人的是人文气息和美食，而不是配套设施。Joo Chiat Road 汇聚了密密麻麻的 Eurasian 和 Peranakan 餐馆、越南餐厅、老式 kopitiam，还有一批新开的酒吧，吸引了年轻人、创意工作者和外籍人士，他们宁愿住在一杯好咖啡楼上，也不在乎有没有购物商场。Thomson-East Coast Line 如今已在 Marine Parade 和 Marine Terrace 设站，这才终于让这一带正式上了地铁版图。不过买家也要看清楚取舍。最好的 shophouses 价格不菲，还受保护条例约束；大多数单位偏小或偏旧；周末和深夜的人流噪音是真实存在的；这里没有自己的购物商场，日常要靠隔壁的 Katong 和 i12。Joo Chiat 与 Geylang 的边缘地带曾有夜生活和红灯区的历史，如今已大多被城市更新所取代，但了解这段背景还是值得的。

适合谁Shophouse 及文化遗产买家与投资者、看重人文气息和美食多过配套设施的创意工作者和外籍人士，以及想要东部中心地带地址的小单位买家。

老实说

好在哪里

  • 真实的人文气息与历史积淀，整个社区都是保护完好的 Peranakan shophouses，更是新加坡首个指定 Heritage Town。
  • 家门口就是新加坡数一数二的美食街，从 Eurasian 和 Peranakan 餐馆，到越南餐厅、老式 kopitiam 和深夜酒吧，应有尽有。
  • 如今已连上地铁网络，Thomson-East Coast Line 在 Marine Parade 和 Marine Terrace 设站，Katong、i12 和 East Coast Park 也都近在咫尺。
  • 毛租金回报率约 3.3%，比核心中央地段更为可观，背后是年轻租客和外籍人士带来的稳定需求。

需要权衡

  • 保护区 shophouses 价格高昂，还附带修缮规定，这类资产绝非随意入手的选择。
  • 大多数单位偏小或偏旧，适合家庭的现代大单位有限，因此更适合单身人士、夫妇和投资者，而非大家庭。
  • 美食和酒吧区少不了周末人流和深夜噪音，部分边缘地带也带有较旧的夜生活名声。
  • 这里没有自己的购物商场，日常购物要靠隔壁的 Katong 和 i12，也没有 HDB 可以退而求其次。
