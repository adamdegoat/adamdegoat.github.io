Telok Blangah

The ridge and harbourfront between Mount Faber and the sea, in District 4, where hillside HDB with sea views sits a short walk from the trophy condos of Keppel Bay.

What it's like

Telok Blangah is really two markets on one slope: affordable-ish hill HDB with a view, and ultra-prime waterfront condos with a private berth.

Telok Blangah runs along the green ridge between Mount Faber and the water, in the Bukit Merah planning area in District 4. The HDB half sits up the hill on roads like Telok Blangah Heights, Crescent and Drive, and the payoff for the climb is the outlook: a lot of these blocks look out over the sea, Sentosa and the city skyline. On the doorstep you get the Southern Ridges walk with Henderson Waves and the Forest Walk, HortPark, Telok Blangah Hill Park and Mount Faber, which is about as much continuous greenery as any HDB estate on the island can offer.

Then there is the other Telok Blangah, down at the shoreline. Keppel Bay is the ultra-prime waterfront end, where Reflections at Keppel Bay, Caribbean and Corals sit on the marina with private berths and resale medians around $2.45m, near $1,864 psf. Up the hill, a four-room resale runs about $932k at roughly $931 psf, and it rents well, close to a 5.0% gross yield. Those two worlds barely overlap. A family buying a hill flat for the view and the walks and an investor buying a waterfront apartment for the berth are shopping in the same postcode for completely different reasons, which is what makes this a hard area to sum up in one number.

Getting out is straightforward. The Circle Line stops right in the estate at Telok Blangah, and HarbourFront next door is a bigger interchange on the Circle and North-East lines, with VivoCity, the cable car and the Sentosa gateway all sitting on top of it. The honest catch is that this is not a shopping-belt address: for a proper mall you lean on VivoCity or head into town, some of the hill blocks are older and a real hike to reach, and at the top end Keppel Bay is expensive and can sit for a while before it sells. You buy here for the ridge, the water and the views, not for a bargain.

Who it suitsBuyers who want hillside HDB with sea and city views and the Southern Ridges on the doorstep, or the Keppel Bay waterfront-condo lifestyle with a private berth.

The honest view

What works

  • Hillside HDB with genuine sea, Sentosa and city views, which few estates can offer at this price.
  • The Southern Ridges, Mount Faber and Telok Blangah Hill Park are on the doorstep, with the Circle Line in the estate and HarbourFront next door.
  • Strong HDB rental demand, close to a 5.0% gross yield for a central location.

Worth weighing

  • The hill HDB and the Keppel Bay waterfront are two markets that barely overlap, so pricing here is hard to read as one number.
  • Keppel Bay is expensive and can be slow to sell, so the top end is not always easy to exit.
  • Some of the hill blocks are older and a real climb to reach, and this is not a central shopping-belt address.

Telok Blangah

District 4 内 Mount Faber 与海之间的山脊海滨地带，山坡上的 HDB 望海而居，步行便可抵达 Keppel Bay 的豪华 condo 群。

这里是什么样

Telok Blangah其实是同一片山坡上的两个市场：价格相对实惠、有景观的山坡HDB，以及带私人泊位的超高端水岸condo。

Telok Blangah沿着Mount Faber与海岸之间的绿色山脊延伸，属于第4区的Bukit Merah规划区。HDB部分在山坡上，Telok Blangah Heights、Crescent和Drive一带，爬上去的回报是视野：很多组屋都望得到大海、Sentosa和城市天际线。出门就是Southern Ridges，有Henderson Waves、Forest Walk、HortPark、Telok Blangah Hill Park和Mount Faber，这片连续绿地在全岛HDB市镇里算是数一数二的。

然后还有另一个Telok Blangah，在海边。Keppel Bay是超高端水岸区，Reflections at Keppel Bay、Caribbean和Corals坐落在码头旁，带私人泊位，转售中位数约$2.45m，接近$1,864 psf。山上四房转售约$932k，大概$931 psf，出租情况不错，毛回报率接近5.0%。这两个世界几乎不交集。一个家庭为了景观和步道买山坡组屋，和一个投资者为了泊位买水岸公寓，邮区相同，理由却完全不同，这也是为什么这个地方很难用一个数字来概括。

出行其实很方便。Circle Line就停在Telok Blangah站，旁边的HarbourFront是Circle Line和North-East Line的交汇站，VivoCity、缆车和通往Sentosa的入口都在上面。说实话，这里不是购物带：要逛正经商场就靠VivoCity或进城，部分山坡组屋屋龄较旧，走上去也有点费力，高端的Keppel Bay价格不低，有时要等一段时间才能成交。来这里买，是为了山脊、海景和视野，不是为了捡便宜。

适合谁想要山坡HDB加海景城景、出门就是Southern Ridges的买家，或者想要Keppel Bay水岸condo生活、带私人泊位的买家。

老实说

好在哪里

  • 山坡上的 HDB，能看到实实在在的海景、Sentosa 和城市天际线，这个价位能做到的组屋区寥寥无几。
  • Southern Ridges、Mount Faber 和 Telok Blangah Hill Park 就在脚边，Circle Line 站就在组屋区内，HarbourFront 也近在咫尺。
  • HDB 租赁需求强劲，这么核心的地段，毛租金回报率接近 5.0%。

需要权衡

  • 山上的 HDB 和 Keppel Bay 滨水区是两个几乎不重叠的市场，所以这里的行情很难用一个数字来概括。
  • Keppel Bay 价格高，有时卖得慢，高端单位不一定容易脱手。
  • 山上有些楼栋较旧，走进去要爬一段坡，而且这里不是购物中心林立的核心商业地段。
