Neighbourhood in Queenstown

one-north

Singapore's planned research and technology district in Queenstown, District 5, home to Biopolis, Fusionopolis and Mediapolis, next to NUS, NUH and the Science Park, with a small pocket of private condos built for the people who work there.

What it's like

A government-planned business park with a ring of condos around it, one-north is bought for one reason above all: steady rental demand from the researchers, engineers and academics who work next door.

one-north is not an old neighbourhood that grew on its own. It is a research and technology district that the government drew up and built in the Queenstown planning area, in District 5, and the names tell you what it is for. Biopolis houses biomedical labs and companies, Fusionopolis holds the infocomm, engineering and media firms, and Mediapolis rounds out the media side. It sits alongside the National University of Singapore, the National University Hospital, the Singapore Science Park and INSEAD, so within a short walk you have a university, a teaching hospital, a business school and thousands of research and technology jobs. The homes here are private condos, and there is effectively no HDB in the immediate pocket, so this is a private market from the first dollar.

What people buy into is the employment base on the doorstep. The condos, One-North Residences, Heritage View, The Rochester Residences and One-North Eden among them, are aimed squarely at the young professionals, researchers, academics and expats who work in the park or at NUS and want to live next to it rather than commute in. That is the whole investment case. When your tenants are drawn from a dense, captive pool of well-paid research and technology staff a few minutes from their front door, demand for rentals stays firm and vacancies stay short. Getting around is easy too, with the Circle Line stopping at one-north and the Buona Vista interchange linking the Circle and East-West lines, plus The Star Vista and Rochester Mall for shopping and the restored black-and-white houses at Rochester Park for dining.

The honest catch is that this is a work-driven district, and it shows. On weekends, once the offices empty out, parts of one-north can feel quiet. There is little heritage or heartland character here, no wet market and hawker centre culture built up over decades, because the whole place was planned and built recently. The condos tend to be compact and investor-led rather than large family homes, and there is next to nothing in the way of landed housing. Buy here for the rental yield and the location next to the park, at around 3.6 percent gross on a median near 1.89 million dollars, and go in knowing you are buying a professional, purpose-built address rather than a rounded, lived-in town.

The honest view

What works

  • Steady, captive rental demand from the researchers, engineers, academics and expats who work in the park or at NUS a few minutes away.
  • Well connected, with one-north on the Circle Line and the Buona Vista interchange onto the East-West line, plus The Star Vista and Rochester for shops and food.
  • Yields are healthy for a central-region address, around 3.6 percent gross, with a condo median near $1.89m and roughly $1,682 psf.

Worth weighing

  • A work-driven district that can feel quiet on weekends once the offices empty out.
  • Little heritage or heartland character, and no built-up wet market and hawker culture, because the whole area was recently planned and built.
  • Condos here are compact and investor-led, with next to no large-family or landed options.

这里是什么样

one-north 是政府规划的商业园区，周围环绕着一圈 condo，买这里的理由其实很简单：隔壁就是工作的研究员、工程师和学者，租房需求稳定持续。

one-north 不是自然形成的老社区，而是政府在 Queenstown 规划区、District 5 规划建造的研究与科技园区，光看名字就知道定位。Biopolis 汇聚了生物医学实验室和公司，Fusionopolis 是资讯通信、工程和媒体企业的聚集地，Mediapolis 则专注媒体领域。园区紧邻新加坡国立大学、国立大学医院、Singapore Science Park 和 INSEAD，步行范围内就有大学、教学医院、商学院，以及数以千计的研究和科技职位。这里的住宅全是私人 condo，附近基本没有 HDB，从一开始就是纯私人市场。

大家买的，是门口这个就业基地。One-North Residences、Heritage View、The Rochester Residences 和 One-North Eden 这些 condo，瞄准的就是在园区或 NUS 工作、想住在旁边省去通勤的年轻专业人士、研究员、学者和外籍人士。这就是整个投资逻辑。当你的租户来自一个密集、稳定的高薪研究和科技人才圈，家门口走几分钟就是上班地点，租房需求自然持续稳固，空置期也短。交通也很方便，Circle Line 在 one-north 有站，Buona Vista 转换站连接 Circle Line 和 East-West Line，购物有 The Star Vista 和 Rochester Mall，用餐可以去 Rochester Park 修复后的黑白洋房餐厅。

说实在的，这里是一个以工作为主的区域，周末一到、办公室空了，one-north 的某些地方会显得相当冷清。这里没什么历史底蕴或组屋区的人情味，也没有几十年积累下来的巴刹和小贩中心文化，因为整个地方都是近年规划建成的。这里的 condo 普遍偏紧凑，以投资为主，不是宽敞的家庭大宅，有地房产几乎找不到。买这里，是为了租金回报和紧邻园区的地理位置，中位数约 $1.89m，毛回报约 3.6%，买之前要清楚，你买的是一个专业、功能性的地址，而不是一个有生活温度、经过时间沉淀的社区。

适合谁在园区或 NUS 工作、想住在旁边的专业人士、研究员和学者，以及看中这个就业基础所带来稳定租房需求的投资者。

老实说

好在哪里

  • 来自在园区或 NUS 工作的研究员、工程师、学者和外籍人士的稳定租房需求，他们的工作地点就在几分钟路程以内。
  • 交通很方便，one-north 有 Circle Line，Buona Vista 还可以转 East-West Line，附近 The Star Vista 和 Rochester 购物吃饭都不缺。
  • 以中区地段来说，租金回报算不错，毛回报率约 3.6%，condo 中位价格接近 $1.89m，单价大概 $1,682 psf。

需要权衡

  • 这一带以上班族为主，到了周末办公楼一散，整个区就会变得比较静。
  • 这一带没什么历史感或组屋区的生活气息，也没有成熟的巴刹和小贩文化，因为整个地区是近年才规划建成的。
  • 这里的 condo 普遍偏小，以投资客为主，几乎没有适合大家庭的大单位，也没有有地住宅可选。
