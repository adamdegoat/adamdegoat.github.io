Bedok

The east's oldest and largest town, built around Bedok Mall and the interchange, with the reservoir on one side, a deep hawker heritage and resale flats that stay in demand.

What it's like

Bedok is old-school east: a big, settled town with cheaper entry prices and food people cross the island for.

Bedok is Singapore's most populous town and one of its oldest, developed from the 1970s and lived-in ever since. The centre pulls everything together at the interchange, where Bedok Mall sits on top of the MRT and bus station, Djitsun Mall is a short walk away, and the Bedok Interchange Hawker Centre and the market blocks around Bedok North feed the whole area. This is a food town first: Fengshan Centre and the Bedok 85 (Fengshan) market are names people travel for, and the everyday coffee shops and wet markets still run daily life here.

The housing leans heavily to resale HDB, and a lot of it is genuinely old, with plenty of blocks now sitting on leases in the 50s and 60s. That is exactly why a four-room flat here has a median around $590k, cheaper than Tampines next door, and why the town keeps attracting first-time buyers and landlords chasing yield. Around the edges you get the private stock: Bedok Residences above the mall, the newer Sky Eden@Bedok, and the older waterside condos out towards Upper East Coast. Bedok Reservoir Road adds a band of newer, pricier flats and condos overlooking the water. It is a mixed, multi-generational town rather than a young-professional enclave.

Transport is solid but not spoiling. Bedok sits on the East-West line, with Bedok North and Bedok Reservoir on the Downtown line covering the northern half of the town, so most of the area is within reach of a train. Changi Airport and the Expo are close, and the ECP and PIE handle drivers. The honest trade-off is the same one the whole east lives with: you are a long way from the city, so the ride into Raffles Place runs a good half hour, and some of the flats you are buying have real lease age to weigh up.

Who it suitsValue-minded buyers and yield-focused landlords who want a mature, food-rich town at a lower entry price, and can live with older leases and an eastern commute.

The honest view

What works

  • One of the highest HDB rental yields in Singapore, around 6.7% on a 4-room, helped by lower flat prices.
  • A genuine food town with the Bedok Interchange Hawker Centre, Fengshan and Bedok 85, plus Bedok Mall right over the MRT.
  • Bedok Reservoir Park and the park connectors give the town real green space and waterfront within walking distance.

Worth weighing

  • A real commute into the central districts, this is the far east.
  • Much of the HDB stock is genuinely old, with many blocks now in the 50s and 60s of remaining lease, which matters for financing and resale.
  • Condos here are a big step up in price for modest yields near 3.1%.

这里是什么样

Bedok 是东部老派的市镇：地方大、生活成熟稳定，入场价更实惠，美食更是让人从岛的另一头专程赶来。

Bedok 是新加坡人口最多的市镇之一，也是历史最悠久的市镇之一，从 1970 年代开始发展，居民世代扎根于此。市镇中心以转换站为核心，把一切串联起来：Bedok Mall 就建在 MRT 和巴士总站上方，Djitsun Mall 走几步路就到，Bedok Interchange Hawker Centre 以及 Bedok North 一带的各个市集供应整个区域的饮食需求。这里是以美食立镇的地方：Fengshan Centre 和 Bedok 85（Fengshan）市场是专程赶来的打卡地，遍布各处的咖啡店和巴刹至今仍是居民日常生活的重心。

这里的住宅以转售 HDB 为主，不少楼龄相当老，很多组屋的剩余地契已进入五六十年阶段。正因如此，这里的四房式单位转售中位数约 $590k，比隔壁的 Tampines 还便宜，也因此持续吸引首次置业者和追求回报的房东。市镇边缘则有私宅项目：Bedok Residences 建在商场上方，较新的 Sky Eden@Bedok，以及 Upper East Coast 一带较旧的滨水 condo。Bedok Reservoir Road 沿线还有一批较新、价格较高、可眺望水景的组屋和 condo。这里是一个多元、多代同堂的市镇，而不是单纯的年轻专业人士聚居地。

交通方面稳定，但不算特别方便。Bedok 位于东西线上，北部由 Downtown 线的 Bedok North 和 Bedok Reservoir 站覆盖，镇内大部分地区都能搭到地铁。Changi Airport 和 Expo 距离不远，ECP 和 PIE 方便自驾出行。不过这里和整个东部一样，有个现实要面对：离市区较远，搭地铁到 Raffles Place 足足要半小时，而且你看中的不少组屋楼龄已不低，剩余地契是需要认真权衡的因素。

适合谁适合注重性价比的买家，以及以回报为目标的房东，想在成熟、美食丰富的市镇以较低入场价置业，并且能接受较旧地契和东部通勤的人。

老实说

好在哪里

  • 新加坡 HDB 租金回报率数一数二的地区，4 房式约达 6.7%，主要得益于这里的组屋价格相对偏低。
  • 地道的美食小镇，有 Bedok Interchange Hawker Centre、Fengshan 和 Bedok 85，Bedok Mall 就在 MRT 站正上方。
  • Bedok Reservoir Park 和公园连道让居民步行即可享受真正的绿地和水岸风光。

需要权衡

  • 这里是新加坡真正的远东区，通勤进中央区是要花时间的。
  • 这里不少 HDB 组屋楼龄确实不小，许多楼栋剩余地契只剩 50 至 60 年，对贷款申请和日后转售都有实际影响。
  • 这里的 condo 价格贵了一大截，但租金回报率却只有约 3.1%，表现普通。
