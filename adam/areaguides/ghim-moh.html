Ghim Moh

A compact, food-famous HDB estate in District 10, wedged between Holland Village, one-north and the Bukit Timah landed belt, with Henry Park Primary at its centre.

What it's like

Ghim Moh is a small HDB pocket that punches above its size on two things Singapore buyers pay for: hawker food and a top primary school.

Ghim Moh is a mature HDB estate in the Queenstown planning area, sitting in District 10 between Holland Village, Buona Vista and one-north, with the Bukit Timah landed belt just to the north. It is small and dense, a tight run of point blocks and slab blocks off Commonwealth Avenue West, and most people know it for one thing before anything else: the food. Ghim Moh Market and Food Centre is a genuine hawker destination that pulls people in from across the island, best known for its curry puffs, chwee kueh and wanton mee. That reputation gives a modest estate an outsized name.

The other engine here is schools. Henry Park Primary sits right inside Ghim Moh and is one of the most sought-after primary schools in the country, and that single fact drives resale demand hard. Families move in and hold on to stay inside the catchment, which is a large part of why million-dollar flats have landed here despite the older stock. Ghim Moh is HDB-led through and through; there is essentially no condo inside the estate itself. The private options are next door, One Holland Village Residences and the launches around Holland V, or the newer stock at one-north and along Sixth Avenue, so a buyer who wants private in this pocket is really shopping the neighbours.

Getting around is a quirk worth knowing. There is no MRT station in Ghim Moh proper, but you are a short walk or ride from three: Buona Vista on the Circle and East-West lines, one-north on the Circle line, and Holland Village on the Circle line. The Ulu Pandan Park Connector runs along the canal beside the flats and links you toward Clementi and the west. The honest trade-offs are the price you now pay for older flats, the lack of a station in the pocket itself, and the size of the estate, which is compact and built up with little room to spread. What you get in return is a central-west address with famous food, a marquee primary school and Holland Village a few minutes away.

The honest view

What works

  • Ghim Moh Market and Food Centre is a hawker destination in its own right, the kind of food address people travel for.
  • Henry Park Primary sits inside the estate, one of the most sought-after primary schools in the country, which keeps resale demand firm.
  • Central-west location with Holland Village, Buona Vista and one-north all a short walk or ride away, plus the Ulu Pandan Park Connector on the doorstep.

Worth weighing

  • You now pay million-dollar prices for older flats, so the entry cost is high for the stock you get.
  • There is no MRT station in Ghim Moh itself; you walk or ride to Buona Vista, one-north or Holland Village.
  • The estate is small and dense, with little room to grow.
  • There is essentially no condo option inside Ghim Moh; for private you look next door to Holland Village or one-north.

这里是什么样

Ghim Moh 是个不大的 HDB 组屋区，但在新加坡买家最愿意掏钱的两件事上，它的存在感远超自身体量：一是熟食，二是顶尖小学。

Ghim Moh 是 Queenstown 规划区里一个成熟的 HDB 组屋区，位于 District 10，夹在 Holland Village、Buona Vista 和 one-north 之间，北面就是 Bukit Timah 的有地住宅带。这里地方小、密度高，沿 Commonwealth Avenue West 排开的是一排排点式楼和板式楼。大多数人提到 Ghim Moh，第一个想到的永远是吃。Ghim Moh Market and Food Centre 是全岛公认的熟食中心，最出名的是咖喱角、水粿和云吞面，从岛上各处吸引食客专程赶来。就是这个名气，让一个不起眼的小组屋区在新加坡打出了远超自身体量的名声。

另一个拉动力是学校。Henry Park Primary 就在 Ghim Moh 里面，是全国最抢手的小学之一，光是这一点，就把这里的转售需求推得很高。很多家庭搬进来之后就扎根不走，为的是留在学校的报名圈里，这也是为什么这里尽管楼龄不小，还是出现了百万元组屋。Ghim Moh 彻头彻尾是 HDB 的天下，组屋区里面几乎没有 condo。想买私宅要看隔壁：One Holland Village Residences、Holland V 周边的新盘，或者 one-north 和 Sixth Avenue 一带的楼盘。所以想在这个地块买私宅，实际上买的是周边邻里的地段。

交通这块有个情况要先说清楚。Ghim Moh 本身没有 MRT 站，但走几步路或骑一小段，就能到三个站：Circle Line 和 East-West Line 交汇的 Buona Vista，Circle Line 上的 one-north，还有同样在 Circle Line 上的 Holland Village。Ulu Pandan Park Connector 沿着组屋旁边的水渠延伸，一路可以连到 Clementi 和西部。说实在的，这里的取舍也很直接：老楼现在卖的价钱不便宜，区内没有地铁站，整个组屋区又小又密，发展空间也不大。但换来的是中西部地段、远近闻名的美食、一所顶尖小学，还有几分钟就到 Holland Village 的便利。

适合谁冲着 Henry Park Primary 来的家庭，还有想要中西部 HDB 地址、出门有美食、紧邻 Holland Village 和 one-north 的买家。

老实说

好在哪里

  • Ghim Moh Market and Food Centre 本身就是个美食目的地，是那种让人专程赶来吃一顿的地方。
  • Henry Park Primary 就在这一带，是全国最抢手的小学之一，也因此撑住了这里的转售需求。
  • 地处中西部，Holland Village、Buona Vista 和 one-north 步行或搭车都几分钟，门口就是 Ulu Pandan Park Connector。

需要权衡

  • 现在这里的旧组屋开价动辄过百万，入场成本不低，但到手的楼龄并不新。
  • Ghim Moh 本身没有 MRT 站，要步行或搭车去 Buona Vista、one-north 或 Holland Village。
  • 这个区域面积小、密度高，没什么发展空间。
  • Ghim Moh 里面基本上找不到 condo；要买私宅，就得看隔壁的 Holland Village 或 one-north。
