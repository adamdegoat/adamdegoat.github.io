Dakota

A food-and-heritage pocket on the Circle Line's east fringe in District 14, home to Old Airport Road Food Centre and the conserved Dakota Crescent, where mature flats sit next to newer riverfront condos.

What it's like

Dakota is bought for the food, the heritage and the Circle Line, an old estate that keeps you close to town without paying town prices.

Dakota sits on the east fringe of the city, wrapped around the Geylang River and the old Kallang Airport ground, and it is known for two things above all. The first is the Old Airport Road Food Centre, one of the most respected hawker centres in Singapore, the kind of place people drive across the island for. The second is Dakota Crescent, one of the oldest SIT and HDB estates on the map, now conserved, with the beloved dove playground still standing at its centre. Put together, this is a food-led, character-heavy address that people grow attached to.

The housing is a real mix. On the mature side you have the HDB flats around Old Airport Road and Dakota Crescent, where a four-room resale runs around $880k, roughly $847 psf. On the private side, a run of newer condos has gone up by the river next to the old flats, names like Waterbank at Dakota and Dakota Residences, with resale around $1.19m or about $1,629 psf. So a settled family in a resale flat and a younger couple in a riverfront condo can live a few streets apart, which is part of the appeal here.

Getting around is the other draw. The Circle Line stops at Dakota and Mountbatten, so you loop across to Marina Bay, Paya Lebar and the east without fighting through town. That central, east-fringe position near the city is what buyers pay for. The honest catches are worth naming. The conserved Dakota Crescent blocks are kept for their heritage and are not for sale, so the estate's character does not come with a flat you can buy. Some of the flats are older, with the shorter leases that go with an estate this age. It also sits inside the wider Geylang planning area, which carries a mixed reputation, and the riverfront condos are compact and priced up for the Circle Line location.

The honest view

What works

  • Old Airport Road Food Centre on the doorstep, one of the most respected hawker centres in Singapore, with the conserved Dakota Crescent and its dove playground nearby.
  • A central, east-fringe address on the Circle Line, with Dakota and Mountbatten stations linking you to Marina Bay, Paya Lebar and the east.
  • A genuine mix of housing, from mature resale flats around Old Airport Road to newer riverfront condos by the Geylang River, so both budgets are covered.

Worth weighing

  • The Dakota Crescent conserved blocks are kept for heritage and are not for sale, so the estate's character does not come with a flat you can buy into.
  • Some of the flats are older, with the shorter remaining leases that come with an estate this age.
  • It sits inside the wider Geylang planning area, which carries a mixed reputation, and the parts nearer Geylang proper are not for everyone.
  • The riverfront condos are compact and priced up for the central Circle Line location, so you pay for the address more than for space.

这里是什么样

买 Dakota 就是冲着美食、历史风貌和环线地铁。这是个老区，让你离市区近，却不用付市区的价格。

Dakota 位于市区东侧边缘，环绕着 Geylang River 和旧 Kallang Airport 用地，最出名的是两件事。第一是 Old Airport Road Food Centre，新加坡最受人尊敬的小贩中心之一，很多人会特地从岛的另一头开车过来。第二是 Dakota Crescent，新加坡地图上最早的 SIT 和 HDB 屋苑之一，现已被列为保护建筑群，那个深受大家喜爱的白鸽游乐场至今仍矗立其中。加在一起，这是一个以美食为灵魂、充满历史韵味、让人住了就舍不得走的地方。

这里的住宅类型很丰富。成熟组屋区这边，Old Airport Road 和 Dakota Crescent 一带的 HDB，四房转售价约 $880k，大概是 $847 psf。私宅这边，河边建起了一批新 condo，紧挨着旧组屋，包括 Waterbank at Dakota 和 Dakota Residences，转售价约 $1.19m，即大约 $1,629 psf。所以，住在转售组屋的成熟家庭和住在河景 condo 的年轻夫妇，可能就隔几条街，这也是这里的魅力所在。

交通便利是另一个吸引力。环线在 Dakota 和 Mountbatten 都有站，可以绕去 Marina Bay、Paya Lebar 和东部，不用穿过市中心。靠近市区的东侧边缘位置，就是买家愿意付钱的地方。不过也有几点要老实说清楚。Dakota Crescent 的保护组屋是为了保留历史风貌而留下来的，不对外出售，所以这里的历史韵味并不意味着你能买到那里的单位。部分组屋楼龄较高，剩余租约相应较短。这里也属于较大的 Geylang 规划区，口碑较为复杂。河边的 condo 单位面积偏小，价格也因环线地铁的位置而被推高了。

适合谁适合热爱美食、想要在环线地铁沿线拥有一个位置居中、有历史韵味地址的买家，不管是成熟组屋还是河景 condo 都可以考虑。

老实说

好在哪里

  • 门口就是 Old Airport Road 小贩中心，新加坡最受推崇的熟食中心之一，附近还有保留完好的 Dakota Crescent 建筑群和鸽子游乐场。
  • 坐落于 Circle Line 上的中部偏东地段，Dakota 站与 Mountbatten 站直通 Marina Bay、Paya Lebar 及东部各区。
  • 住宅类型多元，从 Old Airport Road 一带成熟的转售组屋，到 Geylang River 河畔的较新私人公寓都有，不同预算都能找到合适的选择。

需要权衡

  • Dakota Crescent 的保留建筑属于历史文物，不对外出售，所以这里独特的历史氛围并没有附带一个你可以买入的单位。
  • 部分组屋楼龄较高，剩余租约相应较短，这是这个年代屋苑难免的情况。
  • 这一带属于 Geylang 规划区范围，整体口碑参差，靠近 Geylang 核心地段的部分并不是所有人都能接受。
  • 河畔私人公寓单位较小，价格因 Circle Line 中心地段而水涨船高，买的更多是地段，而不是空间。
