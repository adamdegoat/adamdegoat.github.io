A record number of flats can finally be sold this year. Does that make them cheaper?今年终于有创纪录数量的组屋可以出售。这会让它们更便宜吗？

No fire sale is coming. Newly eligible flats look about 20% pricier per square foot than the rest of their town, but almost all of that gap is the longer lease, not the flat.
