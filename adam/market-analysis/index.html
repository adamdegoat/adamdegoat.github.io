HDB resale has stopped movingHDB 转售价格已经停下来

The median flat is $604 psf, effectively unchanged on the previous three months. Asking prices may still be climbing; what people actually pay is not.中位数为每平方英尺 $604，与前三个月基本持平。挂牌价也许还在涨，但成交价没有。

The market got busier市场更热闹了

10,612 homes changed hands in the last three months against 9,932 in the three before, a 6.8% move in activity even while prices sat still.过去三个月成交 10,612 宗，之前三个月是 9,932 宗，成交量变动 6.8%，而价格几乎没动。

Toa Payoh is the one movingToa Payoh 是涨得最明显的市镇

Flats there went from $713 to $753 psf, up 5.6% across 236 sales, the largest move of any town with real volume. Geylang went the other way, down 5.9%.每平方英尺从 $713 涨到 $753，236 宗成交中上涨 5.6%，是所有有实际成交量的市镇里涨幅最大的。 Geylang 则相反，下跌 5.9%。

Figures are medians computed from residential caveats lodged with URA and resale records published by HDB. A caveat reflects a price a buyer actually agreed, not an asking price. The current month is excluded because caveats lodge with a lag and would read as a fall. Indicative only, not financial advice.数字为市区重建局契约与建屋发展局转售记录计算得出的中位数。契约反映买家实际同意的成交价，不是挂牌价。当月数据不纳入，因为契约登记有滞后，纳入会显得像下跌。仅供参考，不构成投资建议。
