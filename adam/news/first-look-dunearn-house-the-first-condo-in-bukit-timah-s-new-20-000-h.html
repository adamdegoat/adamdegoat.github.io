First Look: Dunearn House, the First Condo in Bukit Timah's New 20,000-Home Estate首探：Dunearn House，武吉知马全新2万户大型社区的首个condo

20,000 homes in new Bukit Timah estate武吉知马新社区总住宅数

Dunearn House is the first private condo to launch within a massive planned estate in Bukit Timah that is slated to eventually hold around 20,000 homes. A recent review walked through the unit layouts, pricing, and what living there might actually look like, alongside a look at how the wider estate is expected to take shape over the coming years.

Being first into a new neighbourhood has its appeal: you get in before the area is fully built up and, in theory, before prices reflect the matured version of what the estate will become. But it also means you are making a decision with the least amount of information. Infrastructure, amenities and transport options in a nascent development tend to improve with each subsequent phase, and so can prices across the estate.

Buyers considering Dunearn House should think carefully about whether being an early mover is genuinely an advantage here. If the broader estate fills in the way developers intend, later launches may come with a more complete picture of what the neighbourhood actually offers. Watching how the next few projects in the same estate are priced and received would tell you a lot more than the first launch alone.

Dunearn House是武吉知马一个大型规划社区里首个推出的私人condo，这片社区预计最终将容纳约20,000套住宅。最近有人实地走访，详细了解了单位的格局、定价，以及在那里生活究竟是什么感觉，同时也看了一眼整片社区未来几年预计如何一步步成形。

率先进入一个新社区自然有它的吸引力：你在这一带还没完全建成之前就入场了，理论上也是在价格尚未反映社区成熟面貌之前布局。但与此同时，这也意味着你是在信息最少的情况下做决定。一个刚起步的发展项目，基础设施、配套设施和交通选择往往会随着后续阶段不断完善，整片社区的价格也可能跟着水涨船高。

有意购买Dunearn House的买家，值得认真想清楚：在这里做早鸟，到底是不是真的占了便宜？如果整片社区按发展商的规划顺利推进，后续推出的项目也许能给你一个更完整的社区图景。密切关注同一社区接下来几个项目的定价和市场反应，往往比只看第一个盘更能说明问题。
