HDB Resale Prices Down Two Quarters Running: What It Means If You Plan to SellHDB转售价连跌两季：计划出售的屋主该怎么看？

2 consecutive quarters of hdb price fallsHDB价格连续下跌季度数

HDB resale prices have now fallen for two quarters in a row. For sellers who were hoping the market would recover and push prices back toward last year's highs before they listed, the data is telling you that window has probably closed for now.

Prices across most estates are still far above where they were before 2020, so the overall picture is not a disaster for sellers. But the direction has shifted, and buyers know it. They are taking longer to decide, making lower offers, and walking away more often than they did a year ago.

The practical advice for anyone planning to sell: price honestly from the start. A flat that sits for months with repeated price reductions ends up attracting lower offers than one that is priced right on day one. Buyers read listing history, and a flat that has been reduced twice signals that the seller is under pressure, which invites even lower bids. Get a realistic valuation, set a number that reflects where the market actually is, and move with some pace.

HDB转售价已经连续两个季度下跌。对于那些一直期待市场反弹、希望价格回升到去年高位再挂牌的卖家来说，数据已经在告诉你：那个时机，目前来看可能已经过去了。

大部分市镇的价格仍然远高于2020年前的水平，所以整体来看对卖家来说并不是灾难。但方向已经改变，买家心里清楚。他们比一年前更慢做决定、出价更低，也更频繁地转身离去。

对任何打算卖房的人，实际的建议是：一开始就老实定价。一个反复降价、挂了好几个月的房源，最终吸引到的出价往往比一开始就定对价格的更低。买家会看挂牌历史，一个降过两次价的单位，释放出来的信号是卖家有压力，反而会引来更低的出价。请人认真做个估值，定一个真实反映当前市场的数字，然后有节奏地推进出售。
