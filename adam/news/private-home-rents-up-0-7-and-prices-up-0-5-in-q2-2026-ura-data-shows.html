Private Home Rents Up 0.7% and Prices Up 0.5% in Q2 2026, URA Data ShowsURA数据：2026年第二季度私宅租金涨0.7%，房价涨0.5%

+0.7% private rent rise Q2 20262026年第二季度私宅租金涨幅

URA's flash figures for the second quarter of 2026 show private residential rents rose 0.7% and prices gained 0.5% over the quarter. Both numbers are positive, but they are modest in a way that would have seemed unthinkable during the rental surge of 2022 and 2023, when quarterly moves of several percentage points were common.

For tenants renting private homes, rents are still edging up, but the rate of increase is something most households can absorb without the kind of financial stress that peaked a couple of years ago. Landlords are still seeing returns move in their favour, just without the dramatic jumps that made yields the centrepiece of every investment property conversation.

For buyers deciding whether to enter the private market, this kind of stability is a reasonable backdrop. Prices are not running away from you the way they were, but they are not correcting either. Borrowing costs remain high enough that the numbers need to be worked through carefully before any purchase makes financial sense, but the urgency to buy before prices run further has largely faded.

URA发布的2026年第二季度初步数据显示，私宅租金按季上涨0.7%，房价上扬0.5%。两项数字虽然都是正增长，但涨幅相当温和，放在2022年和2023年租金飙涨的年代，这种季度涨幅简直难以想象，那时候每季几个百分点才是常态。

对于租私宅的租客来说，租金仍在一点一点往上走，但涨速已是大多数家庭可以承受的水平，不再像前两年高峰期那样让人财务吃紧。对房东而言，回报也还在往好的方向动，只是再没有了当年那种大幅飙升的戏剧感，以前租金回报几乎是每一场投资房产话题的核心。

对于考虑入市私宅的买家来说，这样的稳定态势算是一个尚可的背景。房价不再像以前那样跑得让你追不上，但也没有出现回调。贷款成本仍然偏高，买房前还是要把数字仔细算过，才能确保这笔投资在财务上说得通。不过，那种生怕价格再涨、非得赶快出手的紧迫感，已经基本消散了。

What it means for you对你意味着什么
If you rent privately or own an investment unit, the market is still inching upward but not at the pace that hurt tenants and worried buyers in earlier years.如果你在租私宅，或名下有投资房产，市场仍在缓步向上，但涨势已不像前几年那样让租客吃不消、让买家忧心。
