Condo Prices Had Their Slowest First Half Since 2020: What Buyers Should KnowCondo价格上半年涨势为2020年以来最弱：买家须知

Condo price growth in the first half of 2026 was the weakest of any first half since 2020, the year the pandemic ground activity to a near halt. That is a striking comparison and a signal that the condo market has lost a lot of its earlier momentum.

This does not mean prices are crashing. It means the gap between what sellers want and what buyers will pay has narrowed to a point where deals are harder to close, listings sit longer, and some sellers are having to adjust their expectations before they find a buyer.

For anyone who has been priced out or pressured off the fence over the past few years, this is probably the most comfortable buying environment since 2020. You have more choices, more time to think, and more room to negotiate than at any point in the recent run-up. That does not last forever, but it is real right now.

2026年上半年condo价格涨幅，是近年来同期表现最弱的一次，得追溯到2020年疫情让市场几近停摆的那一年。这个对比相当鲜明，也说明condo市场已经失去了早期的不少动力。

但这不代表价格在崩盘。意思是：买卖双方对价格的预期差距已经收窄到一个点，交易越来越难拍板，房源挂牌时间拉长，有些卖家不得不调整期望才能找到买家。

对过去几年一直被价格逼得犹豫不决的人来说，现在大概是2020年以来最舒适的买房环境了。你有更多选择、更多时间思考，也有更大的空间来谈判，这种状态不会永远持续，但现在确实是真实存在的。
