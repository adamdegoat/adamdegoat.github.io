Boutique Condo Near Kent Ridge Park Sets District 5 Resale Profit Record at $5.4 Million肯特岗公园旁精品Condo以$5.4 million利润创第5区转售纪录

$5.4M record resale profit in District 5第5区转售利润纪录

A unit in a small, low-density condo near Kent Ridge Park changed hands recently at a profit of $5.4 million, setting a new resale profit record for District 5. The development is boutique in scale, which means units come to market infrequently and buyers looking to get in have very few chances to do so.

The combination of scarcity and location is what drives results like this. The Kent Ridge area sits within a well-established part of the west, close to the National University of Singapore, major hospitals, and a cluster of biomedical and research institutions. That creates a consistent base of buyers who are professionally tied to the area and willing to pay for proximity.

For condo owners elsewhere in established districts, this is a useful data point. The general narrative about slowing price growth and a softer market does not apply equally to every project. A rare unit in a limited-supply development with a strong and specific draw for buyers can still produce very large gains, even when the broader market is moving cautiously.

稀缺性加上地段优势，正是造就这一成绩的关键。肯特岗一带地处西部成熟地区，毗邻新加坡国立大学、多家大型医院，以及一批生物医药与科研机构。这为该区带来了一批稳定的潜在买家，他们在事业上与这一带关系密切，也愿意为便利的地理位置支付溢价。

对其他成熟地区的condo业主来说，这是一个值得参考的数据点。整体市场增速放缓、楼市趋软的说法，并不适用于每一个项目。在供应有限、对买家有强烈吸引力的楼盘里，稀缺单位依然可以带来相当大的回报，即便大市正在谨慎前行。
