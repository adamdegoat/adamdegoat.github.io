October 2026 BTO Launch: Six Sites Reviewed, From Bayshore to Sembawang2026年10月BTO推介：六个地点详解，从Bayshore到Sembawang

6 sites in october bto launch10月BTO推介地点数量

The October 2026 BTO launch lines up six sites across a wide spread of locations: Bayshore on the east coast, Caldecott near the central region, Tengah in the west, and Sembawang North in the far north, among others. Six sites means a lot of variety in terms of price, location type, and what kind of life you would be settling into.

Bayshore will attract buyers who want proximity to the waterfront and the Thomson-East Coast Line. Caldecott appeals to those who want a more central address without paying private prices. Tengah suits buyers who are comfortable building a life in a newer town with longer travel times. Sembawang North is likely to be among the more affordable options and suits buyers for whom the lower upfront cost matters most.

If you are planning to apply in October, the ballot is partly luck, but the site choice is entirely yours. Spend the time now reading up on each location, walking the area if you can, and matching the project to how you actually live, not just the price tag. A flat you ballot for out of urgency rather than fit can feel like a compromise for the next 20 years.

2026年10月的BTO推介共有六个地点，分布范围相当广：东海岸的Bayshore、靠近中部的Caldecott、西部的Tengah，以及最北边的Sembawang North，等等。六个地点意味着在价格、地段类型和生活方式上有相当大的选择余地。

Bayshore会吸引希望靠近海滨、同时享有Thomson-East Coast Line便利的买家。Caldecott适合想要较中央地址、又不想付私宅价格的人。Tengah则适合愿意在一个较新市镇扎根、出行时间稍长也无所谓的买家。Sembawang North的价格预计会是几个选项中较实惠的，适合最看重较低首期负担的买家。
