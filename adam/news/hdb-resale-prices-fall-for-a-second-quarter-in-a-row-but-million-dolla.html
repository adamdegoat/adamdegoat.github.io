HDB Resale Prices Fall for a Second Quarter in a Row, But Million-Dollar Flat Sales Hit a New RecordHDB转售价连续第二季下跌，但百万元组屋成交创历史新高

-0.3% hdb resale price change q2HDB转售价变动（第二季）

HDB resale prices fell for the second quarter in a row in Q2 2026, slipping 0.3%. Two consecutive quarters of decline is not a blip. It is a clear sign that the post-pandemic heat has left the broader resale market.

At the same time, million-dollar flat transactions hit a new all-time record. Those two facts sitting side by side can seem contradictory, but they are actually telling a single story: the HDB market is splitting into two very different segments.

If you own a large, well-located flat in a mature estate close to an MRT station, buyers willing to pay top dollar are still there. If your flat is more typical in a less sought-after area, softer prices and more cautious buyers are the reality you are walking into. Where you sit on that spectrum will determine whether the current market feels like a setback or a footnote.

2026年第二季，HDB转售价连续第二个季度下跌，跌幅为0.3%。连跌两个季度，这绝对不是短暂波动，而是一个清晰的信号，疫情后那股热劲，已经从整体转售市场退去了。

与此同时，百万元组屋的成交数量却刷新了历史纪录。这两件事并排摆在一起，看似矛盾，其实说的是同一个故事：HDB市场正在分裂成两个截然不同的板块。

如果你手上有一间面积大、位置好、靠近MRT站的成熟市镇组屋，愿意出高价的买家还是有的。如果你的组屋比较普通、地段也不算抢手，那面对的现实就是价格偏软、买家更加谨慎。你处于哪个位置，决定了当前这个市场对你来说是挫折，还是只是个小注脚。

What it means for you对你意味着什么
The overall HDB market is easing, but high-end flats in prime locations are still fetching record prices, so how you fare as a buyer or seller depends heavily on where your flat sits.HDB整体市场在放缓，但黄金地段的高档组屋依然能卖出破纪录的价格。身为买家还是卖家，结果如何，很大程度上取决于你的组屋在哪里。
