Too Old for a Home Loan and Owns a $1.7M Condo: Should They Right-Size to a Resale HDB?年纪太大贷不到款，手持$1.7M condo：转卖搬进转售HDB合算吗？

$1.7M condo value of right-sizer屋主condo估值

The scenario here is one many older Singaporeans are quietly facing: a fully paid private property worth $1.7 million, no ability to take a new housing loan due to age, and uncertainty about whether staying put or selling and moving to a resale HDB flat is the smarter move.

The numbers favour moving. Selling the condo at $1.7 million and buying even a spacious resale HDB flat in a good location will cost a fraction of that. The difference, potentially several hundred thousand dollars or more, goes into your hands as usable cash rather than sitting locked in bricks and mortar.

The trade-offs are real. You give up the private property lifestyle, and you give up any future upside if the condo market keeps rising. But for someone who can no longer borrow and does not need a large home, freeing up that capital while keeping a roof overhead is a straightforward financial decision. The harder part is often the emotional one of letting go of a home you have held for years.

这个处境其实很多年长新加坡人都在悄悄面对：名下有一套全额付清的私宅，价值$1.7M，但因为年龄原因已无法申请新的房贷，不确定是继续住下去，还是卖掉搬进转售HDB更明智。

从数字来看，搬家是合理的。以$1.7M卖出condo，哪怕买一间地段不错、宽敞的转售HDB，花费也只是其中一小部分。两者之间的差额，可能是几十万甚至更多，会以可用现金的形式回到你手中，而不是锁死在砖墙里动弹不得。

当然，取舍是真实存在的。你会放弃私宅的生活方式，也放弃了condo市场继续上涨的潜在收益。但对于一个已经贷不到款、也不需要大房子的人来说，在保住一个安稳住处的同时释放这笔资本，在财务上是一个清晰的选择。难的往往是情感那关，放下一套住了多年的家，从来都不容易。
