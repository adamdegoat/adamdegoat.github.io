HDB Resale Prices Fall for a Second Straight Quarter, but Million-Dollar Flat Sales Hit a RecordHDB转售房价连续第二个季度下跌，但百万元组屋成交量创历史新高

-0.3% HDB resale price change Q2 2026HDB转售房价变动（2026年第二季度）

HDB resale flat prices slipped 0.3% in the second quarter of 2026, the second quarter in a row that prices have moved in that direction. After years of strong gains, the market is clearly cooling, with the combined effect of government measures and rising supply starting to show up in the numbers.

At the same time, the number of HDB resale flats sold for $1 million or more hit a new all-time high in the same period. Those two facts sitting side by side might seem contradictory, but they tell a coherent story: the broad market is softening while the top end, large flats in desirable locations, is still drawing buyers willing to pay record prices.

What this means for you depends on what you own. If you have a spacious flat in a well-connected town, strong demand from buyers who want exactly that is still very much there. But if your flat is smaller or in a less sought-after area, the wider cooling is the more relevant signal. Pricing carefully and being realistic about what buyers will pay has become more important now than at any point in the past few years.

2026年第二季度，HDB转售组屋价格下跌0.3%，连续第二个季度朝同一方向移动。经历了多年的强劲涨势之后，市场明显在降温，政府降温措施与供应增加的双重效应，正逐渐在数据上显现出来。

与此同时，同一时期内，成交价达到$1百万或以上的HDB转售组屋数量却创下历史新高。这两件事放在一起，乍看之下似乎有些矛盾，但其实说的是同一个故事：整体市场在走软，而高端那一块，也就是地段好、面积大的组屋，依然吸引着愿意以创纪录价格出手的买家。

这对你意味着什么，取决于你手上拥有的是哪种组屋。如果你的单位面积宽敞、交通便利，那类买家的需求依然强劲。但如果你的组屋面积较小，或者地段不那么抢手，整体降温的趋势才是更值得留意的信号。合理定价、对买家的实际承受能力保持清醒认识，在眼下这个时点比过去几年任何时候都来得重要。
