Singapore Condo Prices Had Their Slowest First Half Since 2020新加坡Condo价格上半年涨幅创2020年以来最低纪录

Condo price growth in Singapore during the first half of 2026 was the weakest recorded since 2020, the year the pandemic froze the property market. The data covers January to June and marks a clear break from the pace that defined the market in the years that followed, when annual price gains became the norm rather than the exception.

This is not a price crash. Condos are still getting more expensive, just at a much gentler rate. The cooling measures introduced over the past two years, combined with higher interest rates and buyers who are generally more cautious than they were at the height of the market, are clearly having an effect.

For buyers who were priced out or priced themselves out during the years of sharp increases, this environment is easier to navigate. There is more room to negotiate, less pressure to make fast decisions, and less risk of buying at the very top of a cycle. Sellers need to adjust to this reality: overpricing a unit and waiting for the market to catch up is a less reliable strategy now than it was in 2022.

新加坡condo价格在2026年上半年的涨幅，是自2020年疫情将楼市冻结以来最低的一次。这组数据涵盖一月至六月，明显有别于此后几年市场的走势，那段时期年年涨价几乎成了常态，而非例外。

这不是楼价崩盘。condo还是在涨，只是涨得慢多了。过去两年推出的降温措施，加上较高的利率水平，还有比市场高峰期谨慎许多的买家心态，这些因素合在一起，效果已经开始显现。

对于那些在价格急涨期间被市场淘汰、或者主动退场观望的买家来说，现在的环境明显好应付多了。谈判空间变大了，不需要急着做决定，买在周期顶点的风险也小了。卖家则需要调整心态：把单位定价过高、然后等市场来追上来，这套策略已经不像2022年那么管用了。
