Bayshore Drive Mixed-Use Site Awarded to Frasers Group at $1,323 psfBayshore Drive综合用途地段以$1,323 psf由Frasers集团夺标

$1,323 psf bayshore gls winning bidBayshore政府售地夺标价

The government's Bayshore Drive mixed-use land parcel has been awarded to a consortium led by Frasers Property, which put in a winning bid of $1,323 per square foot per plot ratio. Mixed-use means the development will bring both residential units and some form of retail or commercial space to the site.

Bayshore is a growth area in the east built around the upcoming Bayshore MRT station on the Thomson-East Coast Line. This sale confirms that at least one major developer is confident enough in the area's long-term appeal to commit at a meaningful land price.

For people already living or considering buying in the Bayshore area, this is broadly positive. New homes and ground-floor amenities from this project should materialise within a few years, which adds activity and services to what is still a developing neighbourhood. The flip side: a land cost of $1,323 psf will feed directly into the launch prices of whatever units Frasers eventually brings to market there, so expect new-launch prices in that project to reflect that number.

政府出售的Bayshore Drive综合用途地段，已由Frasers Property领头的财团以$1,323 psf的出价夺标。综合用途意味着这个项目将在同一地点同时开发住宅单位和零售或商业空间。

Bayshore是东部一个正在成长的区域，围绕Thomson-East Coast Line即将开通的Bayshore MRT站而规划。这次地段出售证实，至少有一家主要发展商对这个区域的长期吸引力有足够信心，愿意以可观的地价投入。

对于已经在Bayshore居住或考虑在那里买房的人来说，这整体上是个好消息。这个项目带来的新住宅和底层配套，预计几年内就会落成，为这个仍在发展中的区域增添人气和服务。但另一面是：$1,323 psf的地价，将直接反映在Frasers日后推出的新盘定价上，所以这个项目的新盘价格预计会体现这个数字。
