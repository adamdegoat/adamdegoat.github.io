Private Home Prices Up 0.5% and Rents Up 0.7% in Q2, URA Data ShowsURA数据：第二季私宅价格涨0.5%，租金涨0.7%

+0.7% private rents rise in q2第二季私宅租金涨幅

URA flash estimates for Q2 2026 show private home prices rose 0.5% and private rents climbed 0.7%. These are modest numbers, but they confirm that both the buying and renting markets are still moving in one direction: up.

To put it in perspective, these gains are nothing like the double-digit surges of 2021 and 2022. The pace has slowed sharply, but the floor has not given way. Developers and landlords are not under pressure to cut prices or rents in any meaningful way just yet.

For buyers, prices are not falling, so there is no windfall coming from waiting. For tenants, the relief you were hoping for is not here. What has changed is the urgency: the market is gentle enough now that you can take your time, compare options properly, and push back on asking prices without fear of losing out to the next caller.

URA发布的2026年第二季闪估数据显示，私宅价格上涨0.5%，租金上升0.7%。这两个数字都不大，但都在说明同一件事：无论是买卖市场还是租赁市场，方向依然向上。

对比一下就能看清楚，这些涨幅和2021年、2022年那种两位数的飙升完全不是一个量级。步伐已经明显放慢，但底部也没有塌陷。发展商和房东目前还没有什么压力需要大幅降价或减租。

对买家来说，价格还没在跌，靠等待来捡便宜是不现实的。对租客来说，你期待的那种松动还没到来。但现在的变化在于：市场已经温和到你可以从容看房、好好比较，甚至在讨价还价时不用担心被别人捷足先登。
