Lentor Gardens Residences Sells 270 Units at an Average of $2,350 psfLentor Gardens Residences以平均$2,350 psf售出270个单位

$2,350 psf lentor gardens average sale priceLentor Gardens平均成交价

Lentor Gardens Residences sold 270 units at launch, with buyers paying an average of $2,350 per square foot. That is a solid take-up rate and confirms that appetite for new condos in the Lentor corridor in the north has not dried up, even as broader condo price growth slows to its weakest pace in years.

Lentor has seen several new launches over the past two years and has built a track record of drawing genuine buyer interest rather than just speculative activity. The $2,350 psf average gives the market a concrete reference point for what new-build pricing in that area looks like today.

For buyers comparing new versus resale in the north, that $2,350 psf new-launch average makes nearby resale condos look relatively more affordable. For sellers of resale condos in the area, it sets a ceiling: buyers can see exactly what the new alternative costs, so pricing your resale unit too close to that number without offering something in return will be a tough sell.

对于在北部比较新盘与转售单位的买家，这个$2,350 psf的新盘均价，反而让附近的转售condo看起来相对实惠。而对于在该区出售转售condo的业主，这个数字划定了一个上限，买家可以清楚看到新盘的替代选项价格，所以如果你的转售单位定价太接近这个数字、却又没有提供相应的附加价值，那会是一场艰难的交易。
