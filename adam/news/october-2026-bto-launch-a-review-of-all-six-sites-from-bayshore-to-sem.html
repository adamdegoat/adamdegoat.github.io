October 2026 BTO Launch: A Review of All Six Sites from Bayshore to Sembawang North2026年10月BTO发售：六个地点全面评析，从Bayshore到Sembawang North

6 BTO sites in October 2026 launch2026年10月BTO发售地点

Six BTO sites have been confirmed for the October 2026 launch, covering locations from Bayshore on the east coast to Sembawang North in the north. A thorough review has gone through what each site offers, looking at transport access, nearby amenities and the general feel of each neighbourhood.

Of the six, the sites at Bayshore and Caldecott are expected to draw the most applicants. Both sit close to MRT stations, and Bayshore in particular benefits from its position near the East Coast corridor while Caldecott gives access to a more central, established part of the island. Higher ballot competition at these sites is almost certain.

If you are planning to apply in October, the most useful thing you can do now is work out which of the six sites actually fits your life, not just which one sounds most attractive on paper. The heavily oversubscribed sites may not be worth the odds if your needs could be met just as well by a less competitive one. Understanding the likely ballot ratios before you apply is the difference between a realistic shot and a lottery.
