A launch price only means something next to what resale units nearby actually sell for. Every project below is benchmarked against real caveats within its own area.新盘价格只有对比周边转售的真实成交价才有意义。下面每个项目都以所在区域的真实契约成交作为基准。

Which launch you pick matters more than when选哪个新盘，比什么时候买更重要

A typical launch sells at about 40% over resale nearby, and half of them land between 32% and 57%. But the full range runs from roughly nothing to over 100%. That spread is far wider than any month-to-month move in the market, so the project you choose decides most of what you pay.典型新盘的成交价比周边转售高约 40%，其中一半落在 32% 到 57% 之间。但整体范围从几乎为零到超过 100%。这个差距远大于市场每月的波动，所以你选哪个项目，决定了你多付多少。
